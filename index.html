Doodad support

Privacy policy

Updated November 19, 2026.

Doodad is a local player for self-contained HTML tools. The app has no developer servers, accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

What stays on your device

Doodad stores your tools' HTML, names, appearance, arrangement, saved entries, photos, and audio recordings inside the app's local storage. We do not receive this content. Each tool can access only its own saved content. Network requests and navigation from tools are blocked. Widget shortcuts receive only a tool's name, appearance, and identity, without its HTML, entries, photos, or audio.

Camera and microphone

You choose photos through the system picker. Taking a new photo requires camera permission. A tool can also open Doodad's audio recorder. Recording starts only when you tap Record in that native sheet and grant microphone permission. You can stop or cancel; recording stops when you leave the app or close the recorder. Recordings stay on the device unless you share them. Tools cannot open live camera or microphone streams through browser capture APIs. iOS's media file picker is separate from Doodad's recorder and can offer its own capture options.

Files and clipboard

You choose files to open and when to paste text. Clipboard availability checks inspect content types, not the text itself. iOS's native Paste control reads the text after you tap. Doodad does not send clipboard contents to us.

Sharing

Sharing happens only when you choose it. Include saved data starts off. Turning it on adds saved entries and all photos and audio stored in that tool, including attachments the tool no longer shows. You choose the destination in the iOS Share Sheet. The recipient or provider you choose handles that copy under its own policies. Exported copies are independent and do not synchronize. Files you choose from an external provider may be downloaded by that provider.

Optional tips

Doodad is free, with optional one-time tips through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Tips unlock nothing. Apple processes payments under its own privacy policy. Doodad verifies and finishes transactions on the device; it sends no receipts or purchase history to developer servers. We do not receive your payment details. Apple may provide its standard sales reports to the account holder.

Retention, backup, and deletion

Local tools remain until you remove them or uninstall the app. Doodad does not automatically sync or back up this library. To keep a copy with your progress, photos, and audio, open Share, turn on Include saved data, then choose Save to Files. Resetting saved data or removing a tool deletes its local entries, photos, and audio without changing your original photo library. Uninstalling removes the local library. Copies you exported or shared remain wherever you placed them; manage or delete those separately.

Other apps and services

If you use another app or service to create or update HTML, its privacy policy applies to what you send it. Doodad does not send your tools or saved data to a tool-creation service. Doodad's update instructions include tool source, without the separately saved JSON data, photos, or audio.

Support

If you email us, we use your message and email address to respond and maintain support history. Our email provider processes the correspondence. You can request deletion of support messages. Contact doodad.app@gmail.com with privacy or support questions.
