Privacy policy

Updated November 6, 2026.

Doodad is a local player for self-contained HTML tools. The app has no developer servers, accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

What stays on your device

Doodad stores your tools' HTML, names, appearance, arrangement, and saved data inside the app's local storage. We do not receive this content. A tool can save its own JSON data; it cannot read another Doodad's saved data. Network requests and navigation from tools are blocked.

Files and clipboard

You choose files to open and when to paste text. Clipboard availability checks inspect content types, not the text itself. iOS's native Paste control reads the text after you tap. Doodad does not send clipboard contents to us.

Sharing

Sharing happens only when you choose it. You decide whether a .doodad copy includes saved data and choose the destination in the iOS Share Sheet. The recipient or provider you choose handles that copy under its own policies. Exported copies are independent and do not synchronize. Files you choose from an external provider may be downloaded by that provider.

Optional tips

Doodad is free, with optional one-time tips through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Tips unlock nothing. Apple processes payments under its own privacy policy. Doodad verifies and finishes transactions on the device; it sends no receipts or purchase history to developer servers. We do not receive your payment details. Apple may provide its standard sales reports to the account holder.

Retention, backup, and deletion

Local tools remain until you remove them or uninstall the app. Doodad does not automatically sync or back up this library. You can use Share and Save to Files to keep a separate copy. Removing a tool deletes its local saved data. Uninstalling removes the local library. Copies you exported or shared remain wherever you placed them; manage or delete those separately.

AI services

Doodad does not call an AI service. If you use another app to create or update HTML, that service's privacy policy applies to what you send it. Doodad's update instructions include tool source, not the separately saved JSON data.

Support

If you email us, we use your message and email address to respond and maintain support history. Our email provider processes the correspondence. You can request deletion of support messages. Contact doodad.app@gmail.com with privacy or support questions.
